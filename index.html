🎓 Educational & Technical Qualifications

Candidates must possess any of the following recognized qualifications as prescribed by the Commission:

  • Degree in Engineering: Must hold a Degree in Mechanical Engineering or Automobile Engineering from a recognized University in India.
  • Diploma in Engineering: Must hold a Diploma in Automobile Engineering (3-year or 4-year DAE) awarded by the State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET), Andhra Pradesh, or equivalent.
Note on Equivalency:

Candidates possessing higher qualifications in the direct line of Mechanical/Automobile Engineering are eligible as per official service rules.

🚗 Driving License & HTV Experience Requirements

Transport enforcement requires mandatory heavy vehicle operational expertise:

  • Must hold a valid Motor Driving License with an authorization/endorsement to drive Heavy Transport Vehicles (HTV / HMV).
  • Must have at least 3 years of driving experience with Heavy Transport Vehicles as required by the statutory service rules.
  • Special Provision for Female Candidates: Women candidates who hold only a Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) driving license are permitted to apply, provided they acquire the Heavy Transport Vehicle (HTV) endorsement within two years of appointment.
